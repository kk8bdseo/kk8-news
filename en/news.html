KK8 News and Updates

By KK8 Review BD Editorial · Last verified 2026-09-27 · 18+

kk8 news — a dated record of what has changed on the KK8 platform and what this hub has checked. The newest entries are at the top. Only changes that could be verified directly are written here.

27 September 2026

30 game providers verified

A total of 30 game providers were found on KK8's official platform, including Evolution, Pragmatic Play, JILI, SBOBET and CMD368. An earlier check had shown a shorter list, because the provider section loads late, further down the page. Each provider's category was taken from the platform's own card. The full list is on the games and software page.

A correction on eSports and fishing games

This hub previously said that eSports and fishing sections weren't active. A more detailed check showed that although they have no separate tabs, eSports betting is available in the sports section through E1 Sport, and fishing games in the slots section through JDB. The affected pages have been corrected.

Checks on sites using the KK8 name

Eight domains active in Bangladesh under the KK8 name or a similar one were tested. Two domains use the name "kk8" to run their own login and registration forms, and neither shows KK8's licence number. Details are in the guide to spotting fake sites.

Licence re-checked

Licence number #365/JAZ and eight trust and testing marks in the official platform's footer were checked again. No change.

24 September 2026

The first full check

This hub's first full check established some basic facts: KK8 operates under a Curaçao licence; the official platform's interface is in English; every published promotion amount is in another market's currency; and no separate site or payment page for Bangladesh had been published yet.

What we're watching

  • A separate site for Bangladesh. When it launches, every page on this hub will be updated.
  • Bangladesh payment terms. When minimum amounts and processing times are published, they'll be added to the guides.
  • Offers announced in taka. When an official announcement comes, the bonus review will be updated.
  • New clone domains. These will be checked regularly and the list updated.
