How to work out a bonus's real value

Between what a bonus ad shows and what you actually get stand four conditions. Find all four before you take any offer.

ConditionWhat it meansWhy it matters
TurnoverHow much you must bet in total before withdrawingA big percentage often comes with big turnover
Game weightingHow much bets on each game count towards turnoverLive-game bets often count for less
Maximum withdrawalThe most you can withdraw from the bonusEven a big win can be capped at this limit
ExpiryHow many days you have to meet the conditionsWhen it expires, the bonus and its winnings can be cancelled

A made-up example

The numbers below are invented, purely to show the arithmetic — they are not the terms of any KK8 offer. Say an offer gives a Tk 1,000 bonus, with turnover of 10 times the bonus. Then you'd have to bet Tk 10,000 in total before you could withdraw. If the offer only counts fully on slots and you mostly play live tables, the amount you actually have to bet could be much higher.

That arithmetic shows why a "big bonus" doesn't mean "more profit". For someone who plays little, meeting a big turnover is a burden — and meeting it means playing more than planned.

The types of offer on the platform

On the official platform, promotions fall into five types: a welcome offer for new players, reload offers on regular deposits, rebates on the amount played, small free credit, and limited-time offers around particular events. Of these, rebates are usually the simplest, because they carry fewer complicated turnover conditions. The types are explained in detail on KK8 Bangladesh's promotions page.

Our advice

  • Play without bonuses for the first few weeks. Get to know the platform; there are no conditions on withdrawing.
  • Write down the four conditions before you take a bonus. Turnover, game weighting, maximum withdrawal, expiry.
  • Avoid outside "bonus links". Real offers are only on the promotions page in your own account.
