KK8 Registration Guide — Opening an Account Step by Step

how to register KK8 — a simple question, but the small decisions you make while registering decide later whether withdrawing is easy or gets stuck. This guide explains why each detail is asked for, and where things go wrong.

Three things to prepare before you register

  • Check the correct KK8 website. Make sure you are registering on the intended KK8 platform. Check the footer for licence reference {{licence.number}} before entering your details.
  • Prepare an e-wallet in your own name. Use an e-wallet with the same name as your KK8 account. Matching account details help make future KK8 withdrawals smoother.
  • Keep your NID ready. Prepare a valid NID for KK8 account verification. Completing verification soon after registration helps reduce delays later.

The most expensive mistake is in the first row: a name mismatch. The name you register with must match the name registered on your e-wallet exactly. Changing it afterwards usually isn't easy.

Registration steps

  1. Check the licence number to make sure you're on the official platform.
  2. Click Register or Sign Up.
  3. Enter your details as in the table above — taking particular care with the spelling of your name.
  4. Verify your number with the code sent to your phone.
  5. Straight after opening the account, go to the verification section and submit your ID.

If you want to register quickly, you can go straight to the official link from KK8 Bangladesh's register page.

What causes problems with verification

Most rejected verifications come down to photo quality — blurred text, cut-off corners, or parts hidden by glare. When you take the photo, put your ID on a flat, dark surface and turn the flash off.

An urgent warning: only upload photos of your ID in the upload section inside the platform. If anyone asks you to send documents on WhatsApp, Telegram or email, it's almost certainly a scam.

Questions and answers

It may be difficult to change later. Use your full name when registering, and make sure it matches the name on your e-wallet.

Verification is usually required before withdrawal. Completing it early helps make future withdrawals faster and avoids delays.

Before you enter anything, check that KK8's licence number {{licence.number}} is in the page footer, and read the domain in the address bar letter by letter. If either doesn't match, don't enter your details there.
