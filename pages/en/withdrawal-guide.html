How to withdraw

  1. Make sure all withdrawal requirements are completed.
  2. Log in to the official KK8 platform and open the withdrawal section.
  3. Choose a withdrawal account registered in your own name.
  4. Enter the withdrawal amount and submit the request.
  5. Save the reference number or take a screenshot for your records.

How Long Does It Take?

KK8 aims to process withdrawals within 30 minutes. Times can still vary by payment method, verification status and transaction checks, and delays may occur if further verification is required. Check the withdrawal section for the latest processing information.

First-time withdrawals may take longer because of additional verification. Once you're verified, later withdrawals may be processed more smoothly.

When to withdraw — a simple rule

The biggest obstacle to withdrawing often isn't technical, it's psychological: leave your winnings in the account and there's a tendency to play them again. So set a target before you start — when your balance reaches a certain amount, you'll take part of it out.

Making that decision in advance means you don't have to decide on emotion in the moment you win. For many players, it's the only effective way to hold on to their winnings.

If a withdrawal gets stuck

  1. Check the reason. Review the withdrawal status first. Common causes include pending verification, mismatched account details or incomplete turnover requirements.
  2. Contact support with the details. Give live chat the withdrawal date, amount and reference number so the issue can be checked faster.
  3. Keep your records. Save screenshots, transaction details and support conversations in case you need to follow up later.
  4. Avoid cancelling the request unnecessarily. If the withdrawal is still being reviewed, cancelling it and using the balance again can make the situation more complicated.
