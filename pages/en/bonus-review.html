How to work out a bonus's real value

Between what a bonus ad shows and what you actually get stand four conditions. Find all four before you take any offer.

ConditionWhat it meansWhy it matters
TurnoverHow much you must bet in total before withdrawingA big percentage often comes with big turnover
Game weightingHow much bets on each game count towards turnoverLive-game bets often count for less
Maximum withdrawalThe most you can withdraw from the bonusEven a big win can be capped at this limit
ExpiryHow many days you have to meet the conditionsWhen it expires, the bonus and its winnings can be cancelled
