A simple example

This example only explains how turnover works and isn't based on a specific KK8 promotion. If you receive a BDT 1,000 bonus with a 10× turnover requirement, you would need to complete BDT 10,000 in eligible bets before the bonus conditions are met.

Game weighting also matters. For example, if slots count at 100% but live casino games count at a lower rate, you may need to place more bets to complete the same turnover requirement.

That's why a bigger bonus doesn't always mean better value. Always check the turnover requirement, eligible games and game weighting before claiming a promotion.
