What players in Bangladesh need to know

You can open an account from Bangladesh, but the interface is in English and the published offer amounts are in other currencies. When a separate site for Bangladesh arrives, this hub will update.

Don't decide on the size of an offer

Be wary of any site that claims a specific KK8 bonus amount in Bangladeshi taka — no such offer has been officially announced. How to judge an offer is covered in the bonus review.

Most withdrawal problems can be avoided

The most common reasons a withdrawal gets stuck aren't the platform's fault: incomplete verification, a name mismatch between the account and the mobile wallet, and bonus turnover still outstanding. All three can be sorted out on the day you open the account.

The biggest risk is the wrong address
