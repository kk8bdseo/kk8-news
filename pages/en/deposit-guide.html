KK8 Deposit Guide — with bKash, Nagad and Rocket

KK8 bKash deposit, Nagad, Rocket or Upay — whichever method you use, the rules for depositing safely are the same: only send money from inside your logged-in account, and keep proof of every transaction. This guide explains why, and how.

What's known, and what isn't

bKash, Nagad, Rocket, Upay and Crypto are available as payment options for players in Bangladesh. Deposit rates and limits may vary by payment method, while withdrawal limits, processing times and any applicable fees are shown in the relevant payment section.

Because payment conditions can vary, always check the latest details in your account before making a transaction.

The one rule that protects your money

Only follow deposit instructions shown in the payment section of your logged-in KK8 account. If someone on WhatsApp, Telegram or Facebook gives you a personal bKash, Nagad, Rocket or Upay number and asks you to send money directly, don't make the payment.

Payments made outside the official payment section may not be linked to your KK8 account. If something goes wrong, the transaction may be difficult to trace or recover.

How to deposit safely

  1. Log in through the official KK8 website you have verified.
  2. Go to the deposit or payment section and choose your preferred payment method.
  3. Enter the deposit amount and follow the on-screen instructions carefully.
  4. Save the transaction ID (TrxID) and keep a screenshot of the payment confirmation.
  5. Check that the deposit has been credited to your KK8 balance.

You've sent money, but it isn't in your balance

If this happens, don't send the money again — that's the most common mistake, and it doubles the loss. Instead:

  1. Check in your e-wallet whether the transaction went through.
  2. If it did, contact live chat with the transaction ID, date, time and amount.
  3. Stay in one conversation rather than opening several new requests for the same problem.

Protecting your e-wallet

  • Never share your wallet PIN or OTP with anyone. Legitimate platforms and support teams won't ask for them.
  • Use an e-wallet registered in your own name, to help avoid verification or withdrawal issues.
  • Review your total deposits regularly. Small payments can add up quickly over time.
